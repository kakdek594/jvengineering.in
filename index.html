Company Overview

A practical manufacturing partner for sheet metal components.

JV Engineering was established in 2026 in the Waluj MIDC Area of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra. The company specializes in press-shop operations and the production of sheet metal components.

The manufacturing setup combines power-press production with CO₂ welding capability to support repeat production, component forming and customer-specific requirements based on drawings, specifications and production needs.
